Company Overview

Engineering experience under one roof

SBS Engineering Consultancy PLC is an Ethiopian consultancy based in Addis Ababa, established in 2025. The firm brings highway, structural, hydraulic, geotechnical and contract-administration specialists together to carry projects from route selection and topographic survey through detailed design, cost estimation, tender documents, contract administration and construction supervision.

The firm serves public and private clients on projects of regional, national and international scope.

  • Established 2025
  • Grade IV – Road and Bridge Consultant
  • Senior professionals and technicians
  • Head office in Addis Ababa

Organization

Four Departments. One Integrated Practice.

Design Department

Led by Selamawit Banjaw, this department integrates highway, structural, hydraulic, CADD, and drafting teams to deliver comprehensive, detailed engineering design.

Engineering Contract Management & Project Formulation Department

Led by Salhadin Surur. manages the execution, monitoring, and closure of construction and engineering contracts to ensure compliance with project scopes, budgets, and applicable conditions of Contract.

Contract Administration

Administers executed contracts during construction — managing variations, claims, payment certification, correspondence, and project records to keep delivery compliant with contractual requirements.

Finance & HR

Manages the firm's finances, procurement and personnel.
