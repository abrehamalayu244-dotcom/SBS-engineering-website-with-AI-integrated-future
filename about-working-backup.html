About SBS Engineering Consultancy

Dependable, standards-compliant engineering for roads, bridges and urban infrastructure.

Company Overview

Engineering experience under one roof

SBS Engineering Consultancy PLC is an Ethiopian consultancy based in Addis Ababa, established in 2025. The firm brings highway, structural, hydraulic, geotechnical and contract-administration specialists together to carry projects from route selection and topographic survey through detailed design, cost estimation, tender documents, contract administration and construction supervision.

The firm serves public and private clients on projects of regional, national and international scope.

  • Grade IV – Road and Drilling Consultant
  • Senior professionals and technicians

Mission & Vision

Vision

Our mission is to be a trusted name in Ethiopian infrastructure consultancy recognized for engineering that is technically sound, safe, and built to last.

Mission

To provide accurate survey, sound design, and honest contract administration and supervision that help our clients deliver roads, bridges, and urban infrastructure efficiently and to standard, while developing our people and strengthening the wider engineering profession.
